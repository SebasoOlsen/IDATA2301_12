Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 5/7/2025

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account or interact with the site, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number

You may also provide content in the form of hotel reviews.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely for educational and demonstration purposes, including:

  • Simulating user accounts
  • Displaying user-generated reviews
  • Improving the functionality of the site

No data is sold or used for commercial purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

As this is a school project, we do not store any sensitive information. The data is not used for payments or real transactions, and basic security measures are in place. However, please note that no real payments or sensitive data are stored or processed.

4. Sharing of Information

Your personal information will not be shared with any third parties. The data remains within the StayFinder project.

Cookies and Tracking

StayFinder uses **cookies** to manage user authentication. When you log in, a **JWT (JSON Web Token)** is stored in your browser's cookies. This token is used to verify your identity on future requests, allowing you to stay logged in during your session. The JWT is stored securely in an **HTTP-only cookie** to prevent unauthorized access.

We do not track your browsing behavior or use cookies for advertising purposes. The cookies used for authentication are necessary for the functioning of StayFinder, and you can opt out of certain cookies through your browser settings.

6. Your Rights

Since this is a demo project, you have the right to:

  • View the information associated with your account
  • Delete your account and associated data

For any requests, please contact the project creator (or instructor, if applicable).

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
